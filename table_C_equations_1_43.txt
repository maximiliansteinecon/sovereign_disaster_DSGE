\subsection{Model Equations as Implemented (1--43)}

The Dynare implementation (\texttt{thesis\_model\_v4.mod}) solves the
stationarized system of Appendix~\ref{sec:Stationarization} as 43
equations in 43 endogenous variables. Table~\ref{tab:eq1_43} lists
every equation exactly as coded, cross-referenced to its appendix tag.
Four auxiliary variables (\(CE_t\), \(\mathcal{D}_t\), \(\mathcal{E}_t\),
\(FI_t\)) are numerical-implementation devices with no independent
appendix equation of their own -- their content is folded into the
prose of B.2, B.3, B.3a--c and B.30 respectively; they are listed here
because they are separate Dynare equations, not because they are
separate economic conditions.

\paragraph{Notation.} Two potential ambiguities, resolved once here.
First, the Dynare variable \texttt{Q} is the household stochastic
discount factor \(\mathcal{M}_{t,t+1}\), \emph{not} a price -- Tobin's
Q is the separate Dynare variable \texttt{Qtob}, denoted \(Q_t\) below,
matching Appendix~A.1.8/B.5. Second, timing below follows Dynare's own
convention directly: \(X_t\), \(X_{t-1}\), \(X_{t+1}\) for \texttt{X},
\texttt{X(-1)}, \texttt{X(+1)}. This does not always coincide with the
appendix's own end-of-period capital-dating discussion (\(k_t\) vs.
\(k^n_t\)) -- where it matters, the equation's own appendix tag is the
authoritative cross-reference, not the raw Dynare timing shown here.

\paragraph{Reconciling the count with Table~B.} This table and Table~B
describe the same 43-equation system but count differently: Table~B is
organized by economic content per B-tag (so B.21a--d, four separate
Dynare equations, appear as four rows there too, but some B-tags that
correspond to a single conceptual condition split across several Dynare
lines here are compressed to one row there), and it also lists several
non-independent/reporting equations (Fisher B.30, spread B.28, expected
bond return B.27, EFP schedule A.3.1.3, banker value fn A.3.2.2, bond
and loan clearing) that restate or ground equations counted elsewhere,
which is a documentation choice, not evidence of a different equation
count. Do not treat any row-count mismatch between the two tables as an
error without recounting both by hand first -- I have not independently
reconciled the two tables' totals digit-for-digit, only verified that
every row in \emph{this} table (1--43) matches a real Dynare equation and
a real, correctly-cited appendix tag.

\paragraph{Nothing found missing.} Cross-checked all 43 rows against
their cited appendix tags in \emph{Status Quo - Thesis August 19th.txt}
-- every tag exists and its content matches. Three minor
notation/documentation points, not tag errors, worth knowing if this
table is transcribed further: row 2 (\(CE_t\)) is purely a numerical
auxiliary with no appendix equation to point to, so citing "folds into
B.2" is a description, not a tag; row 7's Dynare-comment cites "(A.1.5/B.2)"
loosely -- it is A.1.5 only, B.2 is where it is \emph{used}, not defined;
and rows 32--35's Dynare-internal ordering (\texttt{OmB, etaB, nuB, lev})
does not match the appendix's own B.21a--d letter order (etaB, nuB,
OmegaB, lambda) -- the table above uses the \emph{appendix's} order and
tags, not the \texttt{.mod} file's internal comment order, so it is safe
to transcribe directly.
